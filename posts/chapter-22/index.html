Chapter 22

July 4, 2026 · 3 min ·   | 

Kodroth smiled widely, a rictus that caused putrid pus to leak from his cheeks and dribble into his beard. It twitched about violently in disgust.

“This isn’t some game,” Brynn shouted, swatting away Kodroth’s hand from his forehead. “How many people have died because of you?!”

“Who’s to say I’m responsible for their deaths? Who’s to say I’m responsible for anything?” Kodroth replied, his smile never fading. “What about you? Did you cause the earthquake? Maybe we are just following our fates.”

Brynn stood up. “Enough! Why are you doing this, Kodroth?”

“Think of all those people who just died. Think of all the pain and violence and despair in this world. It doesn’t have to be that way! We could have a new world! A new world of eternal happiness! No pain, nor violence, nor despair. No death, either. But to have a new world, Brynn … we’ll have to dispose of the old one.”

“You’re insane!”

“I don’t deny it,” Kodroth replied, “but you haven’t seen the prophecies. You haven’t seen the Truth. This world is but one step to a new and better one.”

“You want to sacrifice the people of this world for the next one. I won’t be a part of it.”

“We shall all be restored in the new world, restored by its pristine waters and given new life,” said Kodroth. He gestured at the broken city, his pallid face shining with ecstasy. “Every one of us will be reborn. The victims of this earthquake … Reborn. Themon, Kynan … Reborn. Your parents, Brynn! Reborn!”

Brynn staggered away from Kodroth, shaking his head. He refused to believe.

“Why do you defend those who oppress you?” Kodroth continued. “The Druids, the Kingdom of Galdur, they are all parasites on this land. The Old Ones were the same. It has always been thus. This world is diseased. It is too late to save it. We need to start afresh.”

“No!” Brynn cried. He hid his face in his hands.

“You see it now, don’t you? You see the world as it truly is! You see the suffering! You see the decay! It is rotten to the core. Help me, Brynn! Out of the ashes of this world, we will build paradise. All you have to do is let me speak!”

Brynn jerked awake. The morning’s first rays lit into his face, temporarily blinding him. He rose stiffly and used his tunic to wipe away the cold sweat that had formed on his brow.

Six more days.
